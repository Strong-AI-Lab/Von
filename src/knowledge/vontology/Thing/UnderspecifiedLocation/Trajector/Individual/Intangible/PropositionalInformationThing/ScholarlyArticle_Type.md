# ScholarlyArticle

**Source Concept**: #$ScholarlyArticle
**SubConcept Of**: PropositionalInformationThing

**Description**: ScholarlyArticle is a subclass of "PropositionalInformationThing," representing written works that convey original research, reviews, or theoretical contributions in a specific academic field. Typically peer-reviewed and published in academic journals, these articles are structured to communicate findings, methodologies, and insights to a scholarly audience. Positioned above "Scholarly Work," it serves as a specialized category for precise and formal dissemination of knowledge within the academic community.
