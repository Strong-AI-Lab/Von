# Intangible

**Source Concept**: #$Intangible
**SubConcept Of**: Individual

**Description**: Intangible is a subclass of "Individual," representing entities that lack physical form but exist as discrete, identifiable instances. These include abstract concepts, informational entities, or relationships, such as a mathematical formula, a legal contract, or a specific idea. As non-physical yet distinct entities, "Intangible" provides a framework for categorizing and organizing items that cannot be materially measured but play significant roles in knowledge representation and reasoning.
